# Realistic body provenance

`*.glb` here are generated by `tools/anny`:
- Body geometry and rig: Anny (NAVER LABS Europe, Apache License 2.0), built on
  MakeHuman community assets (CC0 1.0). Synthetic bodies; no person was scanned.
- Motion: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu
  (subjects 02, 137, 80). "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or
  redistributed without permission." The database was created with funding from
  NSF EIA-0196217.
- Clothing: generated procedurally from the body surface by tools/anny.
