# Hero image brief: Part IV — The Modern World

**Accent:** #2C6470 (slate teal)

## Subject

A meeting room after everyone has left: chairs at slightly different angles around a long table, a laptop half-closed, a ring light switched off. The geometry of modern rooms.

## Palette and light

Cool slate and teal, overcast daylight, clean architectural lines.

## Technical

- File: `modern-world.jpg` in this folder (`app/src/main/assets/heroes/`)
- 1600 × 1200 px, JPEG, sRGB, **≤ 200 KB** (quality ~70, progressive)
- The bottom 40% is overlaid by a fade to the page colour and the title, so keep the subject in the upper two thirds and right half.
- Must work under both palettes: the app fades it into Paper (#F7F4EE) or Ink (#121317).
- Until the file exists, the app draws its own hero: the part's accent wash, a large roman numeral and a line figure. Nothing breaks.

## Rules for every hero

- Atmosphere, never instruction. No image may depict a posture, gesture or expression as something to read or imitate; the teaching visuals are the drawn figures, and only them.
- No faces as the subject, no recognisable people, no stock-photo smiles, no suits shaking hands, no brain illustrations, no lie-detector imagery.
- Licensing: commissioned, CC0, or AI-generated with commercial rights. Record the source in `heroes/CREDITS.md`.
- Decorative: the app gives it no content description.
